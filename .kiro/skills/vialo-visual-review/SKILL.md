# Vialo visual review

## Goal

Make Vialo feel like a sunlit editorial city guide joined to a precise scheduling instrument. A judge should see the outcome before reading explanatory copy.

## Review sequence

1. At first glance, identify the primary outcome, optimized route, and next action.
2. Confirm the canvas is light and warm, with cream and white dominant and pastel yellow or pink-plum used selectively.
3. Confirm warm-charcoal text and action colors meet WCAG AA; pastel fills never carry low-contrast body text.
4. Check that the route comparison and scheduled timeline dominate the result page.
5. Remove unnecessary containers, repeated labels, decorative icons, and competing calls to action.
6. Confirm type, spacing, alignment, route strokes, and time columns create identity without ornament.
7. Reject gradients, glass effects, neon color, generic dashboard grids, chat patterns, and excessive cards.

## Evidence

Review screenshots at 360 px and a representative desktop width. State the viewport and UI state for every finding. Classify findings as blocker, major, or polish, then propose the smallest effective correction.
