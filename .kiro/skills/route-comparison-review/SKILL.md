# Route comparison review

## Four-second test

Without supporting prose, a viewer must identify:

1. What changed.
2. Which route is Vialo's result.
3. The measured distance or time saved.
4. Whether the optimized day fits its constraints.

## Integrity checks

- Both routes use the same grounded stops, origin, destination or return rule, travel mode, and map bounds.
- Metrics come from the same directed Routes matrix and real route geometry; values are never fabricated or mirrored.
- The baseline order is identified honestly and any infeasibility is explicit.
- Naive and optimized routes differ by label, stroke pattern, weight, and color.
- Pastel supporting fills do not weaken route contrast or metric legibility.
- Both routes remain visible after the reveal and reduced-motion users receive the final comparison immediately.
- The savings headline sits outside the map and is not obscured by controls or attribution.

## Responsive review

At 360 px, summaries appear before a map at least 280 px tall. On desktop, comparison remains one coherent surface rather than unrelated cards. Capture evidence after the reveal settles.
